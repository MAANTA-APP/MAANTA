# MAANTA marketing agency brief

Last updated: 2026-07-09 · Refresh weekly (Growth track, Step 6).
Scope: one-month pre-launch waitlist campaign. Goal: qualified, segmented list
growth — not generic reach.

## Product in one paragraph

MAANTA is an in-mall deals app launching at BBS Mall, Nairobi. Shoppers discover
nearby deals, claim them in the app, and redeem in person with a code. Merchants
pay only on success — KES 30 per verified redemption from a prepaid wallet — and
mall operators get more tenant activity and visibility into mall traction.

## Audiences (never mixed)

| Audience | Definition | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Shoppers | People who visit or live near BBS Mall | Join the waitlist for launch-day deals |
| Merchants | BBS Mall tenants and nearby businesses | Register interest / book onboarding |
| Mall operators | Mall management and marketing staff (BBS and future nodes) | Talk to us about a pilot |

## Message hierarchy

- **Shoppers**: discover nearby in-mall deals and redeem in person.
- **Merchants**: turn mall footfall into verified redemptions and pay on success.
- **Mall operators**: increase tenant activity and gain better visibility into mall traction.

## What the agency receives

1. This brief + `maanta-project-overview.md`.
2. The three landing-page links and form definitions (`maanta-waitlist-data-schema.md`).
3. The email sequence structure (`maanta-email-segmentation-plan.md`) — agency
   creative must feed these sequences, not replace them.
4. The KPI sheet below.

## KPI sheet and reporting

Weekly report required, split by audience:

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Leads | Waitlist signups by segment |
| CPL | Spend ÷ signups, per segment and per channel |
| Landing conversion | Signups ÷ landing visitors, per segment |
| Ad CTR | Per creative, per channel |
| Audience mix | Shopper : merchant : operator split vs. target |
| Merchant onboarding interest | Merchants replying / booking onboarding |

## Ground rules

- Content calendar reviewed by the founder **before** anything ships.
- All traffic lands on the segmented paths with `source_campaign` tagged —
  no direct-to-homepage or untagged links.
- No pricing claims beyond: KES 30 success fee, pay-on-success, 30-day Elite trial.
- No launch-date promises until the founder confirms the date in writing.
- BBS Mall only; do not advertise other malls or cities.

## Weekly cadence

Friday: founder sends updated priorities + metrics; agency returns the weekly
report. Weakest funnel stage (CTR, landing conversion, or email conversion) is
named and becomes the next week's focus.
