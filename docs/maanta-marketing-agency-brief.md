# MAANTA — Marketing Agency Brief

The launch brief for the agency running the pre-launch waitlist campaign
and launch communications. Read alongside
`maanta-email-segmentation-plan.md` and the three email-sequence docs.

## Objective

**Qualified list-building before the November launch — not broad
awareness.** Success is a segmented waitlist (separate shopper and merchant
lists, plus mall-operator contacts) large and warm enough to make launch
week at BBS Mall visibly busy on both the demand and supply side.

- Campaign duration: **one month**, ending at launch.
- Geography: Nairobi, weighted to BBS Mall's catchment.
- Primary capture: **email and phone**, with role segmentation set by which
  form the person uses.

## The product in one paragraph

MAANTA makes the mall live: shoppers open it to see real deals from real
shops at BBS Mall, claim a deal to get a 6-digit code, and redeem it in
person. Merchants only pay when a customer actually redeems (KES 30 per
verified redemption from a preloaded wallet), can boost deals for KES 500 /
24 hours, and launch merchants get a 30-day Elite trial.

## Audiences and positioning

| Audience | Positioning line | Core message | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shoppers | The mall, made live | Discover real nearby deals and redeem in person — free, instant, no coupons | "Join the shopper waitlist" |
| Merchants | Turn footfall into verified redemptions | Only pay when a customer shows up and redeems; KES 30 per success, nothing otherwise | "Merchants: join the launch list" |
| Mall operators | Activate your tenants, see your mall's data | Tenant engagement + mall-level insight + on-ground activation done for you | "For mall operators" |

Message hierarchy per audience: lead with the benefit line above; support
with one proof point (verified in-person redemption for merchants; live
local deals for shoppers); close with the CTA. Do **not** broadcast one
generic message to all three audiences — every asset is built for exactly
one segment.

## Offers

| Audience | Launch offer |
|---|---|
| Shoppers | Early access at launch; launch-day deal drop for waitlist members |
| Merchants | 30-day Elite trial; founding-merchant onboarding done at their shop |
| Mall operators | Priority pilot slot in the expansion wave; BBS launch data readout |

## Conversion paths

Three landing paths, each hard-setting the segment at the form (see
`maanta-waitlist-data-schema.md` for fields):

1. Shopper waitlist — homepage hero / `/waitlist`
2. Merchant launch list — `/merchants`
3. Mall-operator interest — `/mall-operators`

All paid and organic links must carry UTMs (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`,
`utm_campaign`) — campaign source is stored per signup and reported weekly.

## Channel plan (4-week waitlist push)

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Teaser + shopper waitlist open; organic + paid social (IG/TikTok/Facebook, Nairobi geo); merchant outreach content starts on WhatsApp-shareable formats |
| 2 | Shopper "how it works" content; merchant pricing-transparency content; first on-location BBS Mall creative |
| 3 | Social proof (signup counts, onboarded shop count), shopper referral push, merchant urgency (limited onboarding slots) |
| 4 | Countdown content, launch-day logistics, operator-facing PR/one-pager for partnership conversations |

## KPI starter set

| KPI | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Total waitlist signups | Primary traction measure |
| Shopper / merchant / mall-operator split | Acquisition mix vs. launch needs (supply and demand must both show up) |
| Cost per lead (per segment) | Paid efficiency |
| Landing-page conversion rate | Page–message fit |
| Email open + click rates by segment | Sequence relevance |
| Merchant onboarding intent (score ≥ 50) | Likely launch supply |

Weekly reporting: signups, source performance, audience split,
campaign-to-signup conversion — same format every week so trends are
comparable.

## Constraints and approvals

- Pricing claims must match the product exactly: KES 30 success fee,
  KES 500 / 24h boost, 30-day Elite trial. No invented discounts.
- Legal pages are still in draft — do not link terms/privacy from ads
  until the lawyer-reviewed versions are published (tracked as O5 in the
  readiness tracker).
- Creative approval workflow: agency proposes → founder approves → ship.
  Turnaround expectations to be agreed at kickoff (M5 in the tracker).
