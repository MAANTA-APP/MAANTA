# MAANTA — Mall-Operator Email Sequence

Trigger: contact created with tag `mall_operator` (form signup or manual
add after outreach). Cadence: signup, +4 days, +9 days. Tone: partnership
and data-led — this is B2B relationship-building, not volume marketing.
Small list; personalize where possible (mall name, role). All replies go
directly to the founder.

## Email 1 — Welcome and the MAANTA mall model (immediately)

- **Subject:** MAANTA at {{mall_name}} — the mall, made live
- **Goal:** Frame MAANTA as mall infrastructure, not just a merchant app.
- **Body beats:**
  - Thanks for your interest — brief intro of MAANTA launching at BBS Mall,
    Nairobi this November.
  - The model in one paragraph: shoppers discover live deals inside the
    mall, claim on their phone, redeem in person; every redemption is
    verified at the shop.
  - Why a single-mall focus first: we prove the model deeply at one node
    before expanding — and we publish what we learn to our mall partners.
- **CTA:** Reply to introduce yourself / your role at the mall.

## Email 2 — What operators get (+4 days)

- **Subject:** Tenant activation you can measure
- **Goal:** Sell the operator-specific benefits: traction, data, activation.
- **Body beats:**
  - **Tenant engagement:** MAANTA gives every tenant — not just anchors —
    a way to convert footfall into measured sales visits.
  - **Mall-level data:** verified redemptions produce real numbers on which
    categories, floors, and hours drive activity (aggregated, not
    per-tenant confidential data).
  - **On-ground activation:** our agents onboard tenants at their shops;
    the mall does not need to run anything.
  - **Marketing halo:** launch campaign traffic is mall-branded — deals at
    *your* mall.
- **CTA:** See the launch metrics we'll be able to share.

## Email 3 — Pilot / partnership invitation (+9 days)

- **Subject:** Bringing MAANTA to {{mall_name}}
- **Goal:** Open a concrete conversation: pilot, expansion node, or
  mall-wide rollout.
- **Body beats:**
  - BBS Mall is Node 0; the expansion shortlist is being built now from
    operator conversations.
  - Three ways to engage: (1) watch the BBS results and talk after launch,
    (2) reserve a pilot slot for your mall in the expansion wave,
    (3) discuss a mall-wide rollout with tenant-onboarding support.
  - Offer a 30-minute call with the founder.
- **CTA:** Book a call / reply with a time that works.

## After the sequence

No further automation — every operator contact moves to a manually managed
relationship (founder's pipeline). Tag single-site vs multi-site after the
first call for expansion planning.
