### 3.6 The ten hardening fixes (D196–D205) — what each changed for a shopper

These are the `#280` / `#281` follow-ups. Several are pure race/integrity fixes with real
user-visible consequences, which is the level of explanation requested.

- **D196 — cancel left the screen stuck.** *Before:* cancelling a check-in returned the
  component to `idle`; because the auto-check-in effect is one-shot, a single-claim shopper
  landed back on "Checking you in…" with no request in flight and nothing that could ever
  resolve it. Only a full reload escaped. *Now:* cancelling is its own terminal state —
  "You've left the queue. Your claim is still valid." with "Check in again" and "Show my code".
- **D197 — "you're queued" when nobody was.** *Before:* the route answered `checkedIn` even
  when the queue write silently failed, so a shopper stood waiting while staff never saw them.
  *Now:* every write branch confirms what it actually wrote; a failure returns HTTP 503 with
  "Your arrival was recorded, but we could not add you to the shopper queue. Please scan again."
- **D198 — an earned reward vanished when the flag flipped.** *Before:* the ticket gated the
  Fast Visit panel on the *current* flag alone, so turning the feature off erased an
  already-earned confirmation — while the award RPC kept paying, because it deliberately never
  re-reads the gate. *Now:* the panel renders when the gate is on **or** when this arrival
  already qualified.
- **D199 — queue-jumping by lapsed row.** *Before:* a shopper who scanned the entrance at
  10:00 and reached the till at 10:40 could be revived on the staff list as "arrived 40m ago",
  sorted ahead of everyone who checked in between — in a queue whose entire purpose is
  oldest-first. *Now:* a lapsed row is superseded in place with a **new** arrival time; a live
  row is merely extended.
- **D200 — a silent award failure looked like "you didn't qualify".** PostgREST resolves
  failures as `{ error }` rather than throwing, so the catch never saw them. Now logged.
- **D201 — a malformed id answered 500.** Any signed-in shopper could emit 500s at the
  rate-limit ceiling. Now shape-checked → 400.
- **D202 — a read error hid the Rewards row.** *Before:* `rewardBalance ?? 0` collapsed a
  **read failure** into "you have no rewards" and removed the only link to the page that
  would have said otherwise. *Now:* `null` keeps the row, and `/you/rewards` renders
  "this is a read error, not an empty balance".
- **D203 — a second, weaker countdown.** The reward timer was a private copy of the claim
  countdown with no hour rollover and no non-finite guard — it could reproduce exactly the
  `65:00` string D167 removed from the timer directly above it, and render `NaN:NaN`. Now
  delegates to `formatClaimCountdown`.
- **D204 — the frightening screen on the happy path.** *Before:* after a successful
  verification the served shopper stayed listed and **tappable** on the staff queue for up to
  a full 8-second poll; tapping that stale row produced the full-screen "Code not valid —
  Invalid or already-used code" takeover **for a customer staff had served seconds earlier**,
  on every redemption at a queue-using till. *Now:* the keypad publishes a completion event
  and the panel drops the row immediately.
- **D205** — reward/guard consistency (test-side ratchet).

**Status for all ten: A — live** (the shopper-QR and queue ones), except D198/D200/D202/D203/D205
which are **B — dormant** in effect, because they harden Fast Visit surfaces that do not render
while the flag is off.

### 4.2 The four queue states — loading is no longer "empty"

*Before:* a first load in progress rendered **exactly like "nobody is waiting"**. Staff
glancing down mid-fetch were told the queue was empty by a screen that simply did not know yet.
A failed first load rendered the same silent nothing.

*Now:* four distinct states —
1. **failed first load** → "Couldn't load the shopper queue — retrying. The keypad works as usual."
2. **loading** → `role="status"` "Checking for waiting shoppers…"
3. **empty** → renders nothing (correct: a successful read with nobody waiting)
4. **populated** → the list.

After a *successful* load, later errors keep the last good list rather than blanking a screen
staff are actively working from. **Status: A — live.** (`d27c163` for state 2; `413c8af` for 1/3/4.)

## 10. Current end-to-end journeys

### 10.1 Shopper: discover → claim → travel → scan → wait → present → redeem → result

1. **Discover** — `/feed`, `/browse`, `/map`, `/search`. **Unchanged in the window.** Demo mode
   is ON, so 271 synthetic deals are visible. A demo-data banner renders on the shopper shell.
2. **Deal detail** — YOU PAY, itemised breakdown, and now correctly **"View ticket"** rather
   than "Claim deal" if a live ticket is already held.
3. **Claim** — `claim_deal` RPC, unchanged. Ticket expiry = deal end + 15 minutes.
4. **Ticket** — the code, a **correct** countdown, a **Nairobi** wall-clock line, YOU PAY
   anchored right, w3w chip *if present*, **Navigate** *if resolvable*, and *"Show this screen
   at the counter. / If the timer isn't moving, it's a screenshot."*
   **No Fast Visit panel** (dark).
5. **Travel** — Navigate opens what3words or the in-app map. **Gap:** nothing on this screen
   tells the shopper a MAANTA QR exists at the shop, because the only copy that does is in the
   dark panel.
6. **Scan** — only if they happen to notice a printed sticker. `/qr/<token>` auto-checks them in
   and says "Staff will call your name."
7. **Wait** — no waiting UI beyond that sentence. **No queue position, no "you are 3rd", no
   estimate.** The shopper is told to wait and given nothing to look at.
8. **Present** — "Show my code" returns to the ticket. Staff either tap the queue row or type
   the six digits.
9. **Redeem** — fee disclosure → Confirm → `verify_redemption` → KES 30 debited.
10. **Result** — a neutral, non-celebratory "Code verified" with a check mark, the deal and shop,
    "Redeemed 14:32 today", and the code reference. **No reward card** (dark).

**Where it is inconsistent, thin or unfinished**
- **The QR is invisible to shoppers.** The one loop where the new feature is meant to close —
  *shopper knows to scan* — has no in-app instruction while Fast Visit is dark. The physical
  sticker is doing 100% of the work.
- **Steps 6–7 have no reason to exist for the shopper today.** With no reward and no queue
  position, "check in and wait" is a pure cost. A shopper who scans gets a sentence; a shopper
  who does not gets the identical outcome, faster.
- **No queue position or estimate.** Correct for v1 (over-promising a wait time at a two-person
  counter would be worse), but it is the most obvious gap a shopper will name.
- **Steps 1–3 are entirely synthetic today.** Nothing in `/feed` distinguishes a demo deal from
  a genuine one beyond the shell banner. D189 already bit once — a prospect claimed a synthetic
  deal within ~8 hours.

### 10.2 Merchant staff: open counter → see waiting shopper → call → verify → completion

1. **Open** `/merchant/redeem`. Paused-deal notice if any. Then one of the four queue states.
2. **See** — "Shopper queue / 2 waiting", oldest first, `Amina H. · 20% off lunch · arrived 3m ago`.
3. **Call** — staff read the name off the screen. This is the only thing the queue is for and it
   does it well.
4. **Hand off** — tap the row; the six digits land in the keypad in memory; the identical
   preflight → fee disclosure → Confirm flow runs. Or type them.
5. **Verify** — Confirm is single-flight-guarded. Success screen shows the new balance and the
   server-issued verification time.
6. **Completion** — the served shopper disappears from the queue immediately, and the **Recent**
   strip shows `Amina H. · 20% off lunch — verified just now`, ticking.

**Where it is inconsistent, thin or unfinished**
- **The queue has no empty-state copy.** When nobody is waiting it renders **nothing**. That is
  a deliberate space-saving choice on a small till screen, but it also means staff who have never
  seen the queue populated have no idea the feature exists. A new staff member cannot discover it.
- **Dismiss has no confirmation and no explanation on the staff side.** The shopper is told
  their claim survives; staff are not.
- **3 recent verifications** may prove thin at a busy till.
- **The Fast Visit chip is unreachable**, so the queue row's layout has a branch nobody will see.

### 10.3 Merchant owner: log in → understand MAANTA value → check capacity → create/manage a deal

1. **Dashboard** — lifecycle chip, **Last 7 days** (Claims / Verified visits / Claim→verified % /
   Success fees), **Top deal by verified visits**, **Shop status** (Deal slots `n/limit` +
   "Live now: n", Wallet), **Quick actions** (Redeem / New deal / Top up), the **QR card** with
   Print link, then Recent activity.
2. **Value** — legible and honest. Failed reads show `—`, never a fake zero.
3. **Capacity** — `/merchant/deals` shows Current deals, then **"Ended — archive to free a slot"**
   when applicable, and a footer with `Deal slots n/limit`.
4. **Create** — preflight refuses at cap *before* the wizard and points at Manage deals.
5. **Manage** — Archive appears exactly when it would free a slot.

**Where it is inconsistent, thin or unfinished**
- **Two different capacity numbers on two screens.** The dashboard's "Deal slots" card shows
  `occupiedSlots/limit` with a hint "Live now: n" — the deals list shows `deals.length/limit`.
  They agree today (both count `is_active = true`), but they are two independent queries with two
  different phrasings of the same fact.
- **The dashboard is now long** — seven sections on a phone, with the QR card sitting *below*
  Quick actions and *above* Recent activity. The QR is the least frequently needed and takes the
  most vertical space.
- **"Claims since tracking began"** will be the label for every merchant's first week, including
  Merchant 01's. It is honest; it reads like a caveat on the first thing a merchant sees.
- **Elite is present, priced "Pricing coming soon", and reachable** — a merchant who taps
  "Ask about Elite" reaches support with no number. Correct per the ruling, and it will produce
  a question at Node 0. That question **is the measurement**.

### 10.4 Founder / admin: open operations → understand supply, claims, genuine evidence, interventions

1. **`/admin`** — node switcher → **Needs attention** (deterministic alerts, each with its
   reason) → **Evidence split** (genuine vs demo/mixed, with the D188 caption) → **Operational
   totals (7d)** → **Supply** (Active merchants, **Shopper-visible deals**) → pending approvals
   → **Runtime flags** (read-only) → **Recent admin actions** + Full audit → the scoping footnote.
2. **Genuine evidence** — visible as a number for the first time, with the standing caveat that
   internal E2E activity can still be inside it.
3. **Intervention** — every alert is a link to the page that resolves it.
4. **`/founder`** — loads again, with an honest Claims label.
5. **`/admin/reports`** — no longer capable of printing KES 0 from a failed read.

**Where it is inconsistent, thin or unfinished**
- **The read-failure guard on `/admin` is all-or-nothing.** Any one of fourteen reads failing
  blanks the **entire** console. Honest, but a single flaky count takes away the alerts too.
  The merchant owner dashboard's per-metric model (§5.1) is the better pattern and already exists
  in the codebase.
- **"Genuine-tagged" ≠ external field validation, and only prose says so.** The two-counter rule
  is the most important operating rule at Node 0 and it is a caption, not a number.
- **Attention items are not sorted by severity.**
- **`tier_flags` is empty**, so the plan-limit alert cannot fire yet.
- **The audit reader is a raw feed** — action string, truncated ids, `relativeAgo`, and a JSON
  blob. Boring is correct here, but "admin `4f3a1b2c`…" is not a person's name, and there is no
  filter or search over 100 rows.

## 14. What should NOT be changed before Merchant 01

**Do not touch any of the following.** Node 0 Field Validation Mode is in force: product design
and general engineering are frozen unless field evidence shows a genuine blocker or defect.

1. **Do not enable `fast_visit_enabled`.** It is `false` on production. The migration's own note
   sets the condition: flip it *"only when merchant counter QRs are physically printed at Node 0"*
   — and the print sheet deliberately promises no reward, so turning the flag on mid-run would
   make a wall that promises nothing sit next to an app that suddenly does. Not before Merchant 01.
2. **Do not touch the manual six-digit keypad flow.** Preflight → fee disclosure → explicit
   Confirm is the only money path and every PR in the window preserved it deliberately.
3. **Do not add a second verification path.** The queue row hands off *into* the existing flow.
   That constraint is what makes the queue safe.
4. **Do not put a price on Elite.** Founder ruling 2026-08-24; `pricing-copy.test.ts` enforces it.
5. **Do not raise or explain the KES 300 opening-credit wall to the merchant.** Founder ruling
   2026-08-24: if the operator mentions the balance first, the willingness-to-pay signal is gone
   and cannot be retaken. The "Ended — archive to free a slot" path helps a merchant *understand
   their own capacity*; it must not become a prompt to top up.
6. **Do not redesign the merchant owner dashboard**, however tempting its hierarchy score is.
7. **Do not add queue position, wait estimates, or a shopper-facing queue view** without a queue
   that has ever had two people in it.
8. **Do not run a read-failure sweep across the remaining ~60 screens (D187).** It is deferred by
   an explicit ruling: a 60-file sweep the week of the field run is the risk the freeze exists to
   prevent.
9. **Do not delete or edit the two internal merchant records** (`bf66a041` SKANDI SKAN,
   `67fe233d` E2E Full Sweep Shop) or the internal `success` redemption `72f95ac8`. They are
   technical evidence and are kept.
10. **Do not let demo mode contaminate the measured run.** It is ON by ruling so the marketplace
    can double as a sales-demonstration surface — but it **must be OFF for Merchant 01's own
    onboarding and Shopper 01's claim**, or the evidence is contaminated exactly as D189 was.
11. **Do not count `redemptions.status = 'success'` as field evidence.** Always join both parents;
    even then the answer is 1 and it is internal. External field validation is **0**.
12. **Do not begin the four-agent acquisition phase** — D159 must be resolved first.
