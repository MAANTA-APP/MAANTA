## What MAANTA is

Shops in this mall put deals on MAANTA. You claim one on your phone, walk to the
shop, show them a 6-digit code, and pay the shop as you normally would.

**MAANTA never takes your money.** There is no payment of any kind inside the
app — you pay the shop at the till, in person, the way you always do.

---

## What it costs you

**Nothing.** MAANTA is free for shoppers. No fee to claim, no fee if a code does
not work, no subscription, no card details.

---

## How to use it

1. **Find a deal** — browse, search, or open a link someone sent you.
2. **Claim it.** You get a **6-digit code**.
3. **Go to the shop.**
4. **Show the code to staff.** They type it in and confirm.
5. **Pay the shop** at the till, in full, as normal.

That is the whole thing.

---

## Your code

- It is **6 digits**, in **My deals**.
- It stays valid until the deal ends, **plus 15 minutes** — so you have time to
  reach the counter.
- **Nobody scans your code.** You show it; staff type it. If someone asks to
  scan your phone to take payment, that is not MAANTA.
- Show it to shop staff only.

---

## The QR code at the shop

Some shops have a **MAANTA QR sticker** at the entrance and at the till.

- Open your phone camera and scan it to **check in** when you arrive. Staff can
  then see you are waiting.
- **Scanning does not redeem anything.** It does not pay, it does not use up
  your code, and it does not cost you anything.
- **You still show your 6-digit code at the counter.** That has not changed.
- Scanning is **optional**. If it does not work — bad light, no camera
  permission, a torn sticker — just show your code. Nothing is lost.

---

## When something goes wrong

| What happens | What to do |
|---|---|
| The code will not go through | Read it again from **My deals**, slowly. Let staff try once more |
| The deal has ended | The code cannot be used. Whether the shop still honours the offer is up to them. **You are not charged either way** |
| The shop is busy or the app is slow | Staff will serve you. A problem between the shop and MAANTA is never yours to solve — you should not be kept waiting over it |
| The QR will not scan | Skip it. Show your code |
| Something else | Message us on WhatsApp with the code and the shop name |

**You are never charged by MAANTA, whatever happens.**

---

## Things worth knowing

- **You only pay the shop, in person.** If anything asks you to pay MAANTA, it
  is not MAANTA.
- **Claiming does not reserve stock** unless the shop says so on the deal.
- **A deal can run out.** If everyone has claimed it, you will see it marked as
  fully claimed — you can still look at it, you just cannot claim it.
- **Some deals get paused or end early.** They disappear from the app when that
  happens; a code you already hold stays valid.

---

## What MAANTA knows about you

- Your sign-in details, the deals you claim, and when you redeem them.
- If you scan a shop's QR, **the time you checked in** at that shop. Not your
  location, not your movements.
- Shop staff see your **first name and last initial**, the deal, and the time
  you checked in. They do not see your phone number, your email, or anything
  else.

The full statement is the **Privacy policy** at `/privacy`.
