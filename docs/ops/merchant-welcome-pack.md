## What MAANTA is, in one line

Shoppers in this mall see your deal on their phone, claim it, and bring you a
6-digit code. You verify the code at your counter and they pay you in person.

---

## What it costs you

| | |
|---|---|
| To list a deal | Nothing |
| Per verified redemption | **KES 30** |
| Cut of your sale | **None** — the customer pays you in full, in person |
| Monthly minimum | None |
| A code that expires or is rejected | **Nothing** |

You pay **only** when your staff verify a real customer's code at your counter.
No verified code, no fee.

**Your opening credit.** Your wallet starts with **KES 300** of MAANTA credit —
ten redemptions before you spend anything of your own.

---

## How a sale works

1. You publish a deal from your phone.
2. A shopper claims it and gets a 6-digit code.
3. They come to your shop and show you the code.
4. Your staff type it into MAANTA. **Typing a code charges nothing.**
5. The screen shows you the deal, what the customer pays, and the KES 30 fee —
   *before* anything is charged. You press **Confirm redemption**.
6. The customer pays you at your till, in full, the way they always do.

MAANTA never handles your customer's money.

---

## Your wallet

Your wallet pays the KES 30 fees. You can see every entry — each fee, each
top-up, and the balance after it — in the app under **Wallet**.

Two things worth knowing:

- **An empty wallet does not stop you serving a customer.** Verification always
  goes through. If the balance cannot cover a fee, the fee is recorded as owed
  and settles later. Your customer is never made to wait over a billing matter.
- **An empty wallet does stop you publishing new deals.** Top up before you
  create your next one.

*Topping up:* the payment rails are still being prepared. Your opening credit
covers your first ten redemptions. Ask your MAANTA contact before you rely on
topping up by M-Pesa — do not assume it is available yet.

## When a code will not verify

| The screen says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not found, or already used | Wrong digits, or that code was already redeemed | Ask the customer to re-read it from their phone |
| Expired | The deal ended — codes stay valid for the deal window plus a short grace period | It cannot be verified. Whether you still honour the offer is your decision, and you are charged nothing |
| Anything else | — | Serve the customer, then contact us |

The rule we hold ourselves to: **your customer never waits at your counter
because of a problem between you and us.**

---

## If you think a fee is wrong

Tell your MAANTA contact. An admin reviews the redemption record and resolves it
**within 72 hours**:

- **Upheld** — the redemption is reversed and the KES 30 is credited back to your
  wallet.
- **Rejected** — the fee stands, and you are told why.

We do not quietly adjust balances. Every change is an entry in your ledger that
you can see.
