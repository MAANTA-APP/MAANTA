# MAANTA — photography, filming and content release

# DRAFT — REQUIRES QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE RELIANCE OR USE

**Status:** DRAFT, written 2026-09-01 by a non-lawyer. **Not legal advice.** No
part of this has been checked against Kenyan law.
**Purpose:** the P0 gap recorded in `docs/maanta-documentation-register.md` §8 —
permission to use a pilot participant's words, image and shop in MAANTA's
material. It cannot be obtained retroactively, which is why it is the only P0
with a hard deadline.
**Audience:** founder and counsel (this whole document); field operator (§4 only,
which is the printable page).

---

## 1. The blocker counsel must resolve first

<!-- Stated first because it is the one thing that makes the form below
     unusable as it stands, and it is not a drafting problem. -->

**There is no legal entity to be the counterparty.** MAANTA is not yet
incorporated — the decision is blocked on the November Nairobi trip, and it also
determines which entity contracts with merchants and which is the data
controller.

A release granting rights to "MAANTA" when no MAANTA exists may grant those
rights to nobody, or personally to the founder. Both are bad, and the second is
worse than it looks.

**Two ways forward, for counsel to choose:**

| Option | What it means | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A — name the founder personally**, with an assignment clause transferring the rights to the company on incorporation | The release works today | The founder personally holds a person's image rights in the interim |
| **B — wait for incorporation** | Cleanest | **Merchant 01 runs without any release**, and that material is lost permanently |

**Neither is obviously right, and it is not a documentation decision.** The
form below leaves the party name blank rather than guessing.

> If the pilot runs before this is settled, the honest fallback is: **take no
> photographs, record no quotes.** A run with no content is recoverable. A run
> with content taken without valid permission is not.

---

## 2. Who needs to sign

Three different people can appear in pilot material, and they are **not**
covered by one signature.

| Who | Needs to sign? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Merchant owner** | **Yes** — §4, both parts | Their image, their words, and permission to use the shop name and premises |
| **Merchant staff** | **Yes, individually** — §4 Part B only | Their image is theirs. The owner **cannot** consent on an employee's behalf |
| **Shoppers in shot** | **Yes**, or do not photograph them | Incidental background people in a busy mall are a separate question for counsel |

> **The owner cannot sign for their staff.** This is the mistake most likely to
> be made in the field, because the owner will offer to.

---

## 3. Rules for the operator

- **Get the signature before the camera comes out.** Not after, not "we'll sort
  it later".
- **Read Part A and Part B aloud.** A form signed but not understood is not
  consent.
- **Eastleigh is substantially Somali- and Swahili-speaking.** If the signer is
  not comfortable in English, **do not proceed on a nod.** Use an interpreter
  they trust and record who interpreted. A translated version should be prepared
  before scaled use — flagged for counsel in §5.
- **Never imply that signing is required to take part in the pilot.** It is not,
  and consent given under that impression is not freely given. If they decline,
  the pilot continues exactly as before.
- **Leave them a copy.** Photograph the signed form and file it the same day.
- **If they hesitate, stop.** Record that they declined. That is a complete and
  acceptable outcome.

## 5. Points for counsel

Ordered by how much they change the form.

1. **The party problem (§1).** Everything else is drafting; this is a decision.
2. **Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya).** A photograph of an identifiable person
   is personal data, and this is consent-based processing. Confirm the form meets
   the standard for **freely given, specific, informed and withdrawable**
   consent, and that the withdrawal mechanism is adequate. Note MAANTA's
   published privacy policy already promises erasure "within 14 days" and has no
   machinery behind it (**D144**) — the 14 days above deliberately matches that
   promise rather than inventing a second, different one, but **both may be
   wrong** and should be settled together.
3. **Duration and territory.** Left blank on purpose. Advise a period, and
   whether territory needs stating given the material may be seen anywhere.
4. **The partial-withdrawal wording.** "We cannot always take back every copy" is
   honest and may be legally insufficient. Advise.
5. **Staff consent and the employment relationship.** An employee asked by their
   employer, with the operator present, may not be freely consenting. Is a
   separate signing moment away from the owner needed? This connects to **D170**,
   where a merchant can already enroll a shopper as staff without their consent.
6. **Bystanders.** A busy mall means shoppers in the background. Is a notice at
   the door sufficient, or must every identifiable person consent?
7. **Translation.** Somali and Swahili versions before scaled use — and whether
   a translated form must itself be the signed instrument rather than a reading
   aid.
8. **Minors.** Not addressed. Advise whether the form needs to exclude under-18s
   outright, which is the simplest safe answer.
9. **Retention of the signed forms.** They contain a name, signature and contact
   details. Where are they stored, for how long, and who can see them? A consent
   record is itself personal data.
10. **Interaction with the Merchant Terms.** Should the release stand alone, or
    be referenced there? Standing alone is cleaner — a merchant should be able to
    refuse content use without it touching their commercial relationship.

---

## 6. What this draft deliberately does not do

- **Does not collect an ID or passport number.** A name, signature, date and one
  contact method are enough to identify the signer and let them withdraw.
  Collecting more would be over-collection on a consent form.
- **Does not bundle consent with participation.** They are separate decisions and
  the form says so.
- **Does not use legal register.** It is read aloud to a shop owner mid-shift. If
  counsel needs to add formal wording, the plain-language explanation should stay
  alongside it rather than be replaced.
- **Does not promise complete recall of published material**, because that cannot
  be delivered.
- **Does not cover MAANTA's own staff or contractors.** Different relationship,
  different document.
