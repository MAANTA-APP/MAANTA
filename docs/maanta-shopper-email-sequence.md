# MAANTA — Shopper Email Sequence

Trigger: contact created with tag `shopper`. Cadence: signup, +3 days,
+7 days, launch minus 3 days (calendar-triggered). Tone: energetic,
concrete, Nairobi-local. Every email: one CTA, mobile-first, plain
unsubscribe.

## Email 1 — Welcome (immediately on signup)

- **Subject:** You're on the list — BBS Mall is about to get interesting
- **Goal:** Confirm signup, set expectations, seed the core promise.
- **Body beats:**
  - You're in. MAANTA launches at BBS Mall this November.
  - One line on the promise: real deals from real shops around you, claimed
    on your phone, redeemed in person.
  - What happens next: a couple of emails before launch, then your access
    instructions.
- **CTA:** Follow MAANTA on socials (link to campaign accounts).

## Email 2 — How it works (+3 days)

- **Subject:** Browse. Claim. Walk in. Done.
- **Goal:** Teach the three-step loop so launch-day behavior is instant.
- **Body beats:**
  - Browse live deals at BBS Mall.
  - Claim one — you get a 6-digit code.
  - Show the code at the shop and redeem in person. No coupons to print,
    nothing to pay MAANTA.
  - Optional one-question preference tap: "What deals do you want most?"
    (food / fashion / electronics / beauty / other) → sets shopper interest
    category sub-segment.
- **CTA:** Tell us what deals you want.

## Email 3 — What to expect at launch (+7 days)

- **Subject:** The deals are loading in…
- **Goal:** Build anticipation with concrete deal categories; social proof
  as merchant signups grow.
- **Body beats:**
  - Preview of deal types coming at launch (percentage off, freebies, flash
    deals that run for hours, not weeks).
  - "X shops at BBS Mall are already onboard" (use the real number; skip
    the line if it's not impressive yet).
  - Invite a friend: forward this email / share link.
- **CTA:** Share MAANTA with one friend who shops at BBS.

## Email 4 — Launch countdown (launch − 3 days)

- **Subject:** 3 days. Here's how to get in.
- **Goal:** Convert list to active users on day one.
- **Body beats:**
  - Launch date and time.
  - Exact access instructions (URL / install steps / sign in with your
    phone number).
  - What to do first: browse deals, claim your first code, redeem same day.
  - Launch-day incentive if one is approved (first-claim perk).
- **CTA:** Get ready — save the link.

## After launch

Move contacts who signed in to the product-onboarding flow (out of scope
here); send one "we're live" email to non-converters on launch day +2, then
fold survivors into a general shopper newsletter cadence.
