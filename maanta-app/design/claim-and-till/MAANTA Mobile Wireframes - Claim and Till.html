Mobile Wireframes — Claim & Till

BBS Mall, Eastleigh · mobile 390px · the two moments that make the KES 30 fee real: the shopper claims a code, the merchant redeems it at the till.

Built from the MAANTA wireframe system & Frozen UI tokens. One amber action per screen (#FDBF2D), codes in slashed-zero mono, warnings in rust/flame — never yellow. Screens are labelled with their system IDs (e.g. 8g, 9k). Interactive: tap the Till keypad to enter a code, tap state chips to flip a frame, tap “Claim deal” to open the sheet.
